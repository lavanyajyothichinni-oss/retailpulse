# RetailPulse - AI-Powered Customer Analytics and Demand Forecasting

Zidio Development internship project (Data Science and Analytics).

## What it does
- Cleans about 10 million retail transactions and adds new columns (profit, season, price band, age group, promo flag)
- Segments customers with RFM and KMeans (6 groups)
- Forecasts daily sales with Prophet and LSTM (MAPE 7.66 percent and 10.62 percent)
- Predicts which customers will slow down (churn model)

## Data
Synthetic retail dataset from Kaggle (not included because of its size). Download it and place it in the project folder.

## Notebooks
1. 00_inspect - first look at the data
2. 01_build_dataset - cleaning, joins, new columns
3. 02_customers - RFM, churn risk, KMeans segments
4. 03_forecasting - Prophet and LSTM
5. 04_churn - churn prediction
